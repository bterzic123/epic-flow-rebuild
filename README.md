# Growth Audit — Epic

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/epic-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Epic already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and plan screen rebuilt in Epic's own design. Only the order of the screens changes. The blue, the birth-year keypad, the first-book grid, the book page, the plan cards and the trial timeline are all Epic's.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Create the account after the trial starts | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 02 | Let them pick the first book before the price | CR +10–20% |
| 03 | Put the child and their book on the plan screen | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Show your 4.7 and Editors' Choice where parents pay | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Give a second chance when they close | ARPU +10–15% |

Run together: **CR to purchase up to +25%, ARPU up to +30%.**

## Grounding

Every price, rating, quote, book and feature line comes from Epic's own published material: the App Store listing (4.7 from 519K ratings, Editors' Choice, the 95% parents line, reviews), getepic.com (age-group book picks, weekdays 7am–4pm for schools, the parents page) and Epic's in-app screens (93,99 € annual and 12,99 € monthly, each with a 7-day free trial).

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Epic.
